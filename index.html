Nortex Pay AI — The Agent That Charges the Diaspora and Closes the Books

Nortex Pay AI is a Spanish-speaking agent that lets small businesses in Nicaragua charge their customers and family in the United States through PayPal — and reconciles every payment into their books automatically.

A merchant asks in her own words — «cobrale $50 a mi hijo por la factura A-000123» — and the agent creates the PayPal order, sends the payment link, captures on approval, and posts a balanced double-entry reconciliation: the collection, the PayPal fee booked separately as a cost, and the exchange rate on the payment. Duplicate webhooks are ignored; forged ones are rejected (401). When an instruction is ambiguous, the agent stops and asks — with money, it never guesses.

How it works

  1. Conversational checkout — the merchant speaks Nicaraguan Spanish; a PayPal order is created and the payment link goes to the buyer in the US.
  2. Capture + webhooks — on approval the order is captured; PayPal webhooks are signature-verified and reconciled.
  3. Automatic reconciliation — one payment row + double-entry ledger entries (collection, and the PayPal fee booked separately as a cost) + invoice marked paid, in one transaction. The FX rate used is recorded on the payment.
  4. Collections in the merchant's own words — "¿quién me debe?" summaries and polite reminder drafts with the payment link. Ambiguity always stops and asks.

Money rules (non-negotiable)

  • Amounts travel as strings on the API and as exact Decimal (BigInt) inside Nortex. Never floats.
  • Idempotency keyed on PayPal's capture id: a duplicated webhook is a no-op — never a double charge or double entry.
  • Fail closed: an unverifiable webhook signature is logged and ignored; an amount mismatch is held for manual review.
  • Multi-tenant by construction: every payment only ever touches its own tenant's sale.

Honest scope

PayPal does not operate inside Nicaragua, so the buyer is in the US on the rail they already have. This demo runs entirely on the PayPal sandbox with synthetic data; no real money moves. The agent today produces ready-to-send messages — the WhatsApp Business API transport is the next step.

Built with: TypeScript, PayPal Orders v2 + Webhooks (sandbox), Model Context Protocol, Zod, Vitest — 65 automated tests. Built for the PayPal AI Hackathon 2026.
